## Voice
Warm, playful, short sentences, parent-to-parent. Lead with the WOW moment, not the product. Never preachy about screens. Emojis sparingly (1-3 per caption).

## Hard rules (never break)
1. **Honesty:** all project photos are AI-generated examples. Set the AI flag on every post (instagramData.isAiGenerated, tiktokData.isAigc for video, youtubeData.isAiGeneratedContent, pinterestData.isAiGenerated). Never say "my kid made this" or imply a real child built it. Never invent reviews, testimonials, sales numbers or follower counts.
2. **No fake engagement:** no buying followers, no fake accounts, no comment spam, no automated DMs.
3. **Approval:** nothing publishes until the owner approves the week's batch in chat. Before approval, posts exist only as Metricool drafts (`draft: true`).
4. **Kids' safety:** no real children's faces, names or locations. Safety notes for scissors, balloons, marbles, hot water stay accurate.
5. **Platform rules:** respect each platform's limits and community guidelines; YouTube `madeForKids: false` (content is aimed at parents).
6. **Budget:** stay within 20 Metricool posts per month on the free plan (about 5 per week).
7. **The workbook PDF and templates are the product: never upload them to this public repo.**
